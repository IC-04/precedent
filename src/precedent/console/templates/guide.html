What it does not do yet

  • An invoice run takes about four to six and a half minutes including the Tribunal, and roughly 390 to 610 thousand input tokens. Most of that time is waiting for models.
  • It reads pages through the accessibility tree, not pixels, and runs one task at a time.
  • The company is generated. Your own Gmail and Calendar work through a browser profile you sign into; Slack and other accounts are not connected.
  • The half-finished vendor setup, the split-history question and the prompt-injection invoice are built and graded in tests, but no retained live run shows them.
  • The multi-seed scoreboard and the policy screening model are not wired in.
